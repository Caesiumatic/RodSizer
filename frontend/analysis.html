Analysis Instructions

1. Review the image: the centre panel shows detected particles as boxes. Toggle Show Binary Mask to inspect segmentation, Zoom to look closer, and Hide IDs to unclutter the view; ID colors cycle by decade (0s yellow, 10s magenta, 20s green, 30s cyan, 40s red, ...).

2. Check the scale: the calibration source is shown at the top right. If it is wrong or uncalibrated, click Re-scale and draw a line along the scale bar, or use the Mask slider to fine-tune how aggressively touching particles are separated.

3. Curate the selection (right panel Ledger) — exclude clumps, dirt, or anything that isn't a clean rod:

  • Click a particle on the image to deselect/reselect it (it flashes).
  • Sort any column (e.g. Area ↓) to bring big clumps to the top.
  • ⋮ on a row bulk-selects/deselects everything above or below it.
  • Deselect outliers auto-removes statistical outliers (Tukey 1.5×IQR).

4. Update: press "Update Particles" to generate statistics and histograms from the kept particles.

5. Save & export:

  • Download Excel / PDF: raw data and a report for this image.
  • Add to Folder: confirms these results for the aggregate folder report.
